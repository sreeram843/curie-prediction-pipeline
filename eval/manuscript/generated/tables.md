## Limitations required in every submission

- **partial-sofa:** Challenge data lack GCS, urine output, vasopressor-dose ladders, and a reliable mechanical-ventilation flag. Call the computed score partial SOFA throughout.
- **proxy-label:** SepsisLabel is shifted approximately six hours before the Challenge clinical-onset definition. Use label_start, freeze window_m12_p6, and report 5.97 h as label-relative lead time.
- **emissions-not-pages:** Interruptive counts are emission-level page candidates, not episode-arbitrated or delivered clinician pages. Use 'interruptive emissions' in methods and results.
- **single-public-challenge:** setB is a holdout within Challenge 2019, not an external site. Do not claim MIMIC-IV, prospective, multisite, or clinical validation.
