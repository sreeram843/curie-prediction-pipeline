Clinical Translation Roadmap
Governing Interruptions After Deterministic Deterioration Scoring

1. Current stage (this manuscript)
   Offline, retrospective evaluation of a versioned alert-governance policy after
   deterministic partial-SOFA scoring on the public PhysioNet Challenge 2019
   setA→setB split. Outcomes are alert-stream metrics (detection window,
   interruptive burden, Challenge utility), not patient outcomes.

2. Near-term next studies (no new model training)
   - External retrospective replay on independent ICU cohorts (e.g., MIMIC-IV)
     with protocol labels that are not SOFA-incorporated where possible.
   - Clinician workflow simulation / silent-mode logging of passive vs
     interruptive lane presentations without changing care.
   - Prospective observational shadow-mode study measuring override rate,
     time-to-acknowledgment, and false-page burden.

3. Later translation gates (required before care-facing use)
   - Prospective interventional evaluation with clinician and patient safety
     endpoints, site-specific threshold calibration, and governance change control.
   - Integration with local EHR interrupt policies, audit logging, and rollback.
   - Regulatory and ethics review appropriate to the deployment jurisdiction.

4. Explicit non-claims
   This manuscript does not claim diagnosis, treatment benefit, external
   validity, or readiness for patient care.
